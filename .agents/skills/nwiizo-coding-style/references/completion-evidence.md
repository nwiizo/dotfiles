# Match Claims to Current Evidence

Identify what must be true for the request to be complete and inspect the evidence
for that final state. Reuse current passing checks while their inputs and
dependencies remain unchanged.

A fix needs the original reproduction or an equivalent regression.
Formatting, unit tests, integration, installation, and publication establish
different things. Check required jobs for skipped or missing results.
An agent's final message or a successful process is insufficient on its own.

For installation, inspect the executable actually resolved by PATH and exercise
the changed integration. For requested account checks, distinguish a fake
executable, real identity, and actual service response; keep login codes and
credentials in the user's terminal.

Report failed or unavailable checks with their effect on the conclusion.
Repeat a blocked check after a relevant change. Separate applied edits from
unverified behavior, then finish once the requested result and necessary checks
are supported.
